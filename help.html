Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register for an event?

Go to the Registration page, fill out the form, and click Register.

2. How can I view event photos?

Open the Events page to view the event gallery.

3. How do I save my preferences?

Visit the Preferences page and select your favorite event.

4. How can I find nearby events?

Use the Location page and click "Find Nearby Events".

5. Who do I contact for support?

Use the Contact page or email the community office.
